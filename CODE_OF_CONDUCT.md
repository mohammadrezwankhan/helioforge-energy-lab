# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and evidence-led. Critique code, assumptions and findings rather than people. Welcome questions across disciplines and experience levels. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information or pressure contributors to share client data.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions or restrict participation proportionately. Raise concerns privately with the repository owner through an established trusted channel; do not publish personal details in an issue. A public project must establish a monitored reporting channel before accepting a broad contributor community.
