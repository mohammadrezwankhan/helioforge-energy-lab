# Engineering experimental studies

These twelve studies are bounded, deterministic Python screens for the hybrid, storage, PV and research views. Their defaults are illustrative. The catalogue and per-study Pydantic inputs live in `apps/api/helioforge/engines/engineering_studies.py`; results contain finite JSON metrics, series, tables, details and warnings. The registry remains responsible for attaching model version, source metadata and the evidence hash.

They do not upgrade the existing hybrid capability declarations. No study is an operational controller, calibrated digital twin, compliance decision or independent scientific validation.

## Hybrid

| ID | Calculation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `hybrid-resonance` | A two-degree-of-freedom unit-mass oscillator forms `A = [[0,I],[-K,-C]]`; its eigenvalues are reported as poles. A unit-force frequency sweep solves `(K − ω²I + jωC)q = u`. | Stiffness coupling is a normalized user fraction. The modes are abstract; no measured inverter/network impedances, switching, EMT, protection or SSR certification are present. IEEE's inverter SSR tutorial motivates eigenvalue/interaction screening, not this proxy's calibration: [IEEE tutorial](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9429840/). |
| `hybrid-clipping` | DC-to-AC loading ratio is `Ppv,DC / Pac,rated`; the screen sweeps this ratio against AC-equivalent clipped power. For one fixed hour, inverter DC input is `min(Ppv,DC, Pac,rated/ηinv)`. The remaining DC is split among battery charging and clipping; charging is bounded by battery power and energy headroom. Battery energy increases by the fixed illustrative charge efficiency. | Single-interval, DC-side balance. No discharge, DC/DC voltage window, thermal limit, detailed topology or revenue forecast. NREL SAM is a reference for PV and coupled storage system modeling: [SAM PV models](https://sam.nrel.gov/photovoltaic.html). |
| `hybrid-inertia` | A reduced swing equation integrates a 1 GW default generation trip with fixed-step RK4, bounded derivative-style synthetic inertia and a first-order droop governor. The synthetic inertial response is solved algebraically against the swing equation so it can reduce the first-step RoCoF without a one-step delay. | Aggregate response only; governor droop, reserve, response time, 50 Hz base, 15 s horizon and 20 ms step are fixed and returned in details. Not an EMT, protection or frequency-security study. See the [NREL/NERC fast-frequency-response discussion](https://www.nlr.gov/grid/ieee-standard-1547/bulk-power-reliability-needs). |

## Storage

| ID | Calculation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `storage-degradation` | Three independent terms are reported: illustrative SEI calendar fade proportional to `√years` and a 10 °C doubling factor; a cold-temperature, C-rate-squared plating proxy; and fatigue proportional to equivalent full cycles and depth of discharge. Their sum is capped at 95% capacity loss. | Coefficients are fixed illustrative values, not fitted cell-life parameters. Terms are additive and omit interaction, chemistry, SOC history, protocol and uncertainty. Primary studies model coupled SEI/plating reactions ([JES](https://doi.org/10.1149/1945-7111/ac716a)) and fatigue/equivalent-cycle aging ([IEEE OJIES](https://doi.org/10.1109/OJIES.2020.3015396)); this screen does not implement or reproduce their calibrated models. |
| `storage-stacking` | A bounded SciPy/HiGHS MILP maximizes the explicit interval energy-price margin plus user-specified up/down reserve prices. It tracks charge, discharge, SOC and up/down headroom; enforces mutually exclusive charge/discharge, power and activation-duration energy margins; and fixes terminal SOC to the initial SOC. Energy prices are EUR/MWh, storage power is kW and reserve prices/services use MW. | The sample horizon is 24 synthetic intervals. Prices and service quantities are assumptions, not bids, market data, availability or eligibility. It does not annualize the horizon or model activation energy, penalties, network constraints, taxes or degradation. NREL's [utility-scale battery-storage assumptions](https://atb.nrel.gov/electricity/2024/utility-scale_battery_storage) provide context for storage service/value screens. |
| `storage-synergy` | An exponential low-pass splits the supplied power trace; the flywheel receives the high-frequency residual and the battery receives remaining power. Each device is clipped sequentially by power and available energy using a fixed round-trip-efficiency assumption. Any residual request is reported as unmet. | Requested power samples are limited to ±1 GW, with at most 3,600 samples, to keep the low-pass arithmetic and energy totals finite. This is a reproducible dispatch heuristic, not an optimized controller or measured response model. It omits ramp, thermal, converter and degradation dynamics. The default trace is synthetic. DOE's [energy-storage overview](https://www.energy.gov/oe/energy-storage) provides technology context. |

## PV

| ID | Calculation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `pv-backtracking` | Supplied solar elevation/azimuth is projected onto a local cross-axis plane. A planar row-shadow overlap proxy is calculated from tracker rotation, row pitch and module width. A standard GCR-based angle correction is applied when selected. The slope cross-axis component is `atan(tan(slope)·sin(azimuthslope−azimuthaxis))`. | This uses a local slope projection and a 2D row plane. It is not the complete NREL slope-aware algorithm, an annual energy estimate, or a detailed terrain/shading model. See the primary [NREL slope-aware backtracking report](https://docs.nrel.gov/docs/fy20osti/76626.pdf). |
| `pv-agrivoltaic` | Shoelace area and edge lengths are calculated from a simple non-self-intersecting local polygon. The usable-area estimate subtracts `perimeter × setback`. Crop PAR is an area-average screen `PARopen·[1−GCR·(1−transmission)]`. | The setback is not an exact polygon offset. PAR omits hourly sun, diffuse light, crop physiology, spectral effects, access and yield data. The [NREL agrivoltaics report](https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy24osti/88444.pdf) is contextual research, not validation of this screen. |
| `pv-albedo` | Twelve monthly front and rear-ground irradiation values feed `Efront = Pdc·PR·Gfront` and `Erear = Pdc·PR·bifaciality·Gground·albedo·rear-view-factor`. Annual energy is the sum of the monthly rows. | Monthly scalar values and view factor are user assumptions. No ray tracing, module mismatch, temperature, spectral response or representative weather-year claim. NREL's [bifacial modeling toolkit](https://www.nrel.gov/pv/pv-bifacial-irradiance-toolkit) describes the fuller geometry and irradiance problem. |

## Research

| ID | Calculation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `research-vibration` | A mean-removed Hann-windowed real FFT reports a one-sided amplitude spectrum and top bins. The dominant frequency is compared between the early and late half-records. Empty input produces a deterministic, seeded synthetic two-tone chirp/noise example. | Requires evenly spaced samples; half-record resolution is coarser than full-record resolution. Peaks are descriptive, not fault diagnosis. The DFT and spectral-analysis references are in the [SciPy signal guide](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/tutorial/signal.html). |
| `research-epd` | Each unique supplier-stage record contributes `quantity × emission factor` when its factor is present and its stage is selected. Supplier, unit, evidence ID, data quality, missing fields and stage totals remain visible. Only the first occurrence of each duplicate record ID is counted. | Each record quantity is bounded to 0–1e12 declared units and each factor to 0–1e6 kgCO₂e per declared unit. Select and document suitable units; these broad finite input limits prevent overflow and do not validate an inventory's scale. The default ledger is synthetic. Missing factors are not imputed. The arithmetic does not verify an EPD, product-category rule, system boundary, assurance or corporate Scope 3 treatment. The [GHG Protocol Category 1 guidance](https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/standards/Scope3_Calculation_Guidance_0.pdf) describes the supplier-specific quantity × factor method and evidence/data-quality context. |
| `research-scr` | Classical single-infeed screening ratio is `SCRscreen = Ssc/Pplant`. A balanced small-drop estimate is `(R·P + X·Q)/VLL²`; the example fixes `X` to 0.3 Ω/phase and exposes `R`. | SCR alone cannot establish stability. The first-order drop assumes balanced three-phase conditions and small series drop. IEEE hardware experiments demonstrate that SCR and X/R can affect GFM/GFL voltage stability differently ([IEEE PESGM paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11225025/)); broader limits of classical SCR screening are discussed in this [IEEE distribution-strength study](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11693070/). |

## Validation implemented here

The focused pytest file checks the 12-study catalogue and defaults, output JSON finiteness and series lengths, strict/finite input rejection, hand-computed EPD/SCR cases, clipping energy balance and recovery limits, reserve schedule power/SOC constraints, flywheel/battery power and energy accounting, tracker shade response, polygon shoelace/setback values, bifacial annual/monthly reconciliation, known FFT frequency, synthetic repeatability and bounded swing response. Passing these software tests is not independent model validation.
