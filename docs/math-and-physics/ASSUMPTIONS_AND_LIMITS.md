# Assumptions and limits

## Model and evidence boundary

HelioForge's bundled examples are synthetic or explicitly unverified user inputs. A reproducible seed, an optimizer status, a small residual and a source digest establish different properties; none establishes measurement quality, calibration, authority or approval. Preserve those labels in the UI, CSV, JSON, Markdown and any downstream report. [apps/api/helioforge/provenance.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/helioforge-energy-lab/blob/fb42eb4d9b0c87e692710e8e867ab5190ea831a7/apps/api/helioforge/provenance.py), [apps/api/helioforge/studies.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/helioforge-energy-lab/blob/fb42eb4d9b0c87e692710e8e867ab5190ea831a7/apps/api/helioforge/studies.py), [apps/web/src/result-provenance.ts](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/helioforge-energy-lab/blob/fb42eb4d9b0c87e692710e8e867ab5190ea831a7/apps/web/src/result-provenance.ts), [apps/web/src/evidence.ts](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/helioforge-energy-lab/blob/fb42eb4d9b0c87e692710e8e867ab5190ea831a7/apps/web/src/evidence.ts).

| Model family | Intended question | Material limits |
|---|---|---|
| Short storage MILP | What is the minimum modeled cost for this supplied short period and cyclic battery? | Perfect input foresight; aggregate AC-side energy; no inverter dynamics, taxes, degradation fitting or invented service revenue |
| Hybrid screen | Can a declared topology/reference and supply priority serve target load under a scenario? | Greedy hourly dispatch, free terminal SOC, synthetic profiles; control labels are applicability gates rather than dynamic controller simulation |
| Terminal | How do 11 bounded PV/battery/grid inputs change a 24-hour recurrence? | Different profile and scope from hybrid/MILP; finite sampled sweep, free end energy, no thermal/voltage/frequency dynamics |
| Annual tariff policy | How does a transparent policy accumulate 8760 explicit hours? | Non-leap/no-DST calendar, threshold dispatch, no global optimum |
| Annual optimizer | What constrained annual objective can the modeled MILP achieve? | Perfect foresight and fixed calendar; confirmed negative-export baseline flaw can contaminate attributed savings |
| Finance/PV/acquisition | How do declared costs, generation and financing assumptions affect screening metrics? | Distinct project/equity/CFADS bases; bracket-limited IRR and endpoint omission; no executed contracts, tax advice or investment recommendation |
| Forecast/reliability | What follows from declared stochastic assumptions and finite seeded trials? | Uncalibrated processes; conditional/finite-sample results; zero observed events do not prove zero risk |
| Electrical/control/thermal studies | What does a bounded numerical model do at its chosen inputs? | Idealized components, selected dynamics, finite grid/tolerance and local equilibrium; no hardware/safety/grid-code certification |
| Evidence/council | What structure, arithmetic and declared provenance can be inspected? | Digests/signatures do not verify a person or document truth; local checklist is not an external expert panel |
| Teaching companion | How do selected conceptual controls alter its illustration? | Its fixed battery-power proxy has no battery energy ledger and must not be presented as a dispatch study |

## Comparison discipline

Before comparing cases, align engine/version, source/data identity, units, calendar, horizon, service requirement, price basis and initial/terminal energy. Hybrid's comparison contract checks provenance and horizon but does not make differently served loads or different end inventories economically equivalent. Terminal's conditional Pareto filter matches total load and served energy but still displays stored-energy change separately. Two low costs are not comparable when one case leaves substantial demand unserved. [apps/web/src/hybrid-core.ts](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/helioforge-energy-lab/blob/fb42eb4d9b0c87e692710e8e867ab5190ea831a7/apps/web/src/hybrid-core.ts), [apps/web/src/terminal-engine.ts](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/helioforge-energy-lab/blob/fb42eb4d9b0c87e692710e8e867ab5190ea831a7/apps/web/src/terminal-engine.ts), [apps/api/helioforge/engines/workflow_studies.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/helioforge-energy-lab/blob/fb42eb4d9b0c87e692710e8e867ab5190ea831a7/apps/api/helioforge/engines/workflow_studies.py).

The default Terminal island case costs EUR 0 because it imports nothing, while leaving approximately 62.425696 kWh unserved. That result is useful as a diagnostic, not evidence that island operation dominates the grid-connected reference. The native [worked labs](../developer/WORKED_EXAMPLES_AND_LABS.md) retain both outcomes.

## Data and legal meaning

The six market codes select illustrative defaults and configured connector zones. Their labels do not establish current tariffs, regulatory eligibility or project economics. Negative-price settlement studies are scenario logic with unverified country context. Regulatory publications carry timestamps/text requiring accountable review before they become an effective-date timeline. Geographic coordinates, telemetry and extracted documents retain their provenance and authenticity limitations. [apps/api/helioforge/catalog.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/helioforge-energy-lab/blob/fb42eb4d9b0c87e692710e8e867ab5190ea831a7/apps/api/helioforge/catalog.py), [apps/api/helioforge/engines/decision_studies.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/helioforge-energy-lab/blob/fb42eb4d9b0c87e692710e8e867ab5190ea831a7/apps/api/helioforge/engines/decision_studies.py), [apps/api/helioforge/integrations.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/helioforge-energy-lab/blob/fb42eb4d9b0c87e692710e8e867ab5190ea831a7/apps/api/helioforge/integrations.py).

Parcel setback geometry is not a legal boundary determination; crop response is an illustrative light-response curve. Off-gas voting and thermal reaction models are not commissioned alarms or a validated safety system. A learning signature and completed self-check do not confer certification. Keep the existing security, licensing and citation text intact.

## What the 2026-11-19 review establishes

All canonical source payloads and modes were verified. Numerical core and all 59 registered study handler/schema contracts were reviewed with source locators. Python/JS/TS parsing and interface extraction cover their complete enumerated source sets; generated records, media and historical evidence have explicit narrower dispositions. The exact-pin local API suite, browser Node suite, asset checks, build and bounded labs passed within their stated scopes. The actual local Chromium capability probe failed with a permission error, so new browser interaction, screenshots and visual accessibility were not executed. Docker, external providers, device access and real-world calibration were not run. Current candidate CI, independent candidate QA, merge and live content are separate gates. [Verification ledger](../maintainer/VERIFICATION_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md).

The known annual comparator and IRR endpoint findings remain visible source debt, with reproductions and acceptance criteria in the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md). This documentation work preserves the numerical implementation and records those limits rather than presenting a clean-test result as evidence that no defects remain.
