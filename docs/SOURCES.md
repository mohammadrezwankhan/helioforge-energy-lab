No live feed is implemented. A hyperlink is not a connector, eligibility determination, tariff assumption or permission to scrape. Before adding a data integration, verify licence/terms, access credentials, units, timestamps, timezone, missing-data policy, revision handling and reproducible caching. Before encoding a rule, identify the legal instrument, relevant provision, effective period, customer class, geography and independent reviewer. Never substitute a press headline for an operative rule.

All included project names, tariffs, weather-like curves, pilot evidence and emissions factors are illustrative. No public dataset has been copied and relabelled as a live feed. Private professional-history details are excluded from the public demonstration.
